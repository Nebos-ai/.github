## 3. The agency: Office for IT and eGovernment (ITE)

- **Founded:** 2017, by government decree. **Reports directly to the Prime Minister.** ([ite.gov.rs](https://www.ite.gov.rs/tekst/en/124/office-for-it-and-egovernment.php))
- **Mandate:**
  - consolidate state IT resources and connect government information systems
  - design and run eGovernment and state administration IT infrastructure
  - set ICT standards for state bodies
  - run the government computer network and internet services
  - run centralised e-services, including the eUprava portal
- **Infrastructure it runs:**
  - State Data Centre, Kragujevac
  - Belgrade data centre
  - State Cloud
  - National AI Platform and its supercomputers
- **Commercial arm:** **Data Cloud Technology** is a state-owned company that sells State Data Centre capacity to private clients. ([CorD](https://cordmagazine.com/business/success-stories/danilo-savic-data-cloud-technology-new-strengthin-digital-sovereignty/))
- **Main partners:**
  - **UNDP:** long-standing implementing partner
  - **Vendors:** NVIDIA, Eviden/Atos, Mistral AI, Microsoft, Huawei, e&
  - **EU:** EuroHPC
  - **Domestic:** Ministry of Science, Innovation Fund, Science Fund, Institute for AI R&D of Serbia (IVI)

### The wider policy picture
- **AI Strategy 2025–2030** was adopted in early 2025. The Ministry of Science proposed it; ITE delivers the infrastructure pillar (the National AI Platform). It follows Serbia's first AI strategy (2019/2020–2025). ([srbija.gov.rs](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/vest/en/241045/strategy-for-ai-development-until-2030-adopted.php), [IVI](https://ivi.ac.rs/en/news/serbia-adopts-artificial-intelligence-development-strategy-for-2025-2030/))
- **AI Law:**
  - A working group was set up in 2024, modelled on the **EU AI Act**.
  - The draft was targeted for March 2025.
  - Full implementation is aimed for end-2027 and would include a national AI oversight body.
  - Status at the time of writing: not confirmed as adopted.
  - ([Serbian Monitor](https://www.serbianmonitor.com/en/serbia-preparing-artificial-intelligence-law/), [CMS](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/ai-regulation-scanner/serbia))
- **GPAI:**
  - Serbia co-chaired the Global Partnership on AI and hosted the **GPAI Summit in Belgrade** (3–4 Dec 2024).
  - 44 members plus the EU adopted the **Belgrade Ministerial Declaration**.
  - Serbia became lead chair for 2025.
  - ([srbija.gov.rs](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/specijal/en/236368), [OECD.AI](https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/documents/gpai-belgrade-ministerial-declaration))

## 4. AI projects the Office is running or involved in

### 4.1 National AI Platform and the supercomputers (Kragujevac)

Hosted at the State Data Centre. Free access for universities, institutes and startups in science and technology parks; access is granted through public calls. ([ite.gov.rs](https://www.ite.gov.rs/tekst/en/35/national-platform-for-artificial-intelligence-ai.php), [OECD OPSI](https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/ai-supercomputing-platform/))

| Phase | Hardware | Performance | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **#1** (Dec 2021) | 4× NVIDIA DGX A100: 32 GPUs, 1,024 threads, 150 TB | 5 PFLOPS AI | about €2 m | Live since 2021 |
| **#2** (first upgrade) | 6× NVIDIA DGX H200: 48 GPUs, plus 120 TB | 32 PFLOPS AI | about €5 m | Brought online in spring 2026 ([SeeNews](https://seenews.com/news/serbia-launches-second-supercomputer-at-kragujevac-data-centre-1294061)) |
| **#3: National AI Factory** (Eviden) | BullSequana XH3000: about 300 GPUs, 2.5 PB storage | Up to 25 PFLOPS (HPC figure) | **€50 m contract** (Apr 2025) | Due for installation by end-2026. Once installed, Serbia would have the largest AI supercomputing cluster in SE Europe by early 2027. ([Eviden](https://eviden.com/insights/press-releases/eviden-signs-a-50-million-euro-contract-to-build-serbias-national-ai-factory/), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/serbian-govt-signs-contact-with-eviden-for-50m-supercomputer/)) |

### 4.2 National large language model for Serbian (sovereign LLM)
- **14 Jan 2026:** Jovanović and Chamber of Commerce president Marko Čadež signed an agreement with the **Serbian Chamber of Commerce (PKS)** to start building the model. ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/lifestyle/pks-i-kancelarija-za-it-i-eupravu-pocele-saradnju-na-izradi-velikog-jezickog-modela-za-srpski-jezik/), [ai.gov.rs](https://www.ai.gov.rs/vest/sr/2365/srbija-razvija-veliki-ai-jezicki-model-za-srpski-jezik.php))
- **29 Apr 2026:** a cooperation protocol with **11 data and content holders** was signed at eGovernment House, with UNDP support. ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/serbia/news/cooperation-protocol-development-national-large-language-model), [SeeNews](https://seenews.com/news/serbia-advances-plans-to-develop-llm-1293957)) The 11 are:
  - National Library of Serbia
  - University Library "Svetozar Marković"
  - RTS and RTV (public broadcasters)
  - Official Gazette
  - TANJUG news agency
  - Politika
  - JeRTeh (Society for Language Resources and Technologies)
  - ReLDI Centre
  - Institute for the Serbian Language of SANU (Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts)
  - Institute for AI R&D (IVI)
- **Principles:** data is stored at the Kragujevac data centre and the model is trained on Serbian supercomputers.
- **Timeline:** Jovanović said first results would come by end-2026. Independent observers expect a working model in 2027–28. ([Netokracija](https://www.netokracija.rs/srpski-llm-225848))

### 4.3 AI assistants on eUprava (the eGovernment portal)
- **Phase 1, informational assistant** (launched June 2025):
  - answers questions in everyday language and sends users to the right e-service
  - built on an **OpenAI** model, but the platform can switch language models
  - handles more than 45,000 queries a month
  - ([Tanjug](https://www.tanjug.rs/ekonomija/srbija/169470/mihailo-jovanovic-gradanima-na-portalu-euprava-dostupan-digitalni-asistent-baziran-na-ai/vest), [Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/drustvo/ai-digitalni-asistent-implementiran-na-portalu-euprava/))
- **Phase 2, transactional assistant:**
  - booking appointments, filling in forms, automating processes
  - first use case: AI booking of **ID card and passport appointments** at police stations, announced for **end of September 2026**
  - ([Tanjug](https://www.tanjug.rs/ekonomija/srbija/276424/mihailo-jovanovic-krajem-septembra-uz-pomoc-vestacke-inteligencije-zakazivanje-termina-za-zamenu-lk-i-pasosa/vest), [Espreso](https://www.espreso.co.rs/vesti/drustvo/1617456/od-kraja-septembra-zakazivanje-termina-za-dokumenta-uz-ai-asistenta))
- **"Radoslav", the social-protection AI assistant** (live from **22 Jul 2026**):
  - available 24/7 in Serbian
  - covers 5 cash benefits: social assistance, child allowance, two care allowances, and a special cash benefit
  - built under the UN joint programme "Da kliknemo sa građanima" (UNICEF, UNDP, FAO) with two ministries and ITE
  - ([ite.gov.rs](https://www.ite.gov.rs/vest/sr/12760/srbija-dobila-digitalnog-asistenta-za-socijalnu-zastitu-zasnovanog-na-vestackoj-inteligenciji-.php), [RTV](https://www.rtv.rs/sr_lat/drustvo/poceo-sa-radom-radoslav-asistent-vestacke-inteligencije-za-pomoc-gradjanima-u-oblasti-socijalne-zastite_1730923.html))

### 4.5 SAIFA: Serbian AI Factory Antenna (EuroHPC)

- Consortium:
  - ETF Belgrade (coordinator)
  - Faculty of Sciences, University of Kragujevac
  - Institute for AI R&D (IVI)
  - Mihajlo Pupin Institute
  - Institute of Molecular Genetics and Genetic Engineering (IMGGE)
  - Institute of Physics Belgrade
  - **Office for IT and eGovernment**
- ([ETF](https://www.etf.bg.ac.rs/en/news/2026/05/project-saifa-serbian-artificial-intelligence-factory-antenna-launched), [hpc.rs](https://www.hpc.rs/news/saifa-project-officially-launched), [Pupin](https://www.pupin.rs/en/research-and-development-projects/european-rd-projects/saifa/))

## 6. Criticism and risks to know about
- **Environment and energy:** independent outlets (*Vreme*, *Nova/Radar*, *Kragujevačke*) describe the Kragujevac data centre as an "ecological bomb". It sits next to a landfill that has operated for decades without a permit, and at full load it would use about 123 GWh a year. ([Vreme](https://vreme.com/drustvo/superkompjuter-data-centar-kragujevac-ekologija/), [Radar](https://radar.nova.rs/ekonomija/drzavni-data-centar-u-kragujevcu-ai/))
- **Privacy and surveillance:** experts are concerned about centralising state data and about Chinese, US and EU vendors all being involved. ([BIRN](https://birn.rs/pametna-srbija-vestacka-inteligencija/))
- **Vendor and geopolitical mix:** Huawei/China, NVIDIA/US, Eviden and Mistral/EU, and e&/UAE all have a stake. The US State Department has commented on Belgrade–Beijing tech ties. ([Serbian Monitor](https://www.serbianmonitor.com/en/serbias-future-on-test-state-department-on-the-rapprochement-between-belgrade-and-beijing/))
- **Political exposure:** he is a political appointee associated with SNS. Leadership could change if the government changes.
